# BMad Technical Research

## When to Use
Use during analysis or early planning when technical uncertainty could materially change the plan.

## Goal
Turn technical unknowns into explicit trade-offs and constraints for downstream BMad artifacts.

## Procedure
1. Define the technical questions that matter for feasibility or scope.
2. Research alternative architectures, tools, and delivery constraints.
3. Compare trade-offs, integration risks, performance implications, and operational burden.
4. Record what is known, unknown, and what requires validation later.
5. Feed the findings into `bmad-create-prd` and `bmad-create-architecture`.

## Completion Output
Report options considered, chosen leaning, major technical risks, and recommended next workflow.
