# BMad Market Research

## When to Use
Use during analysis when the opportunity, competitors, or buyer landscape needs evidence before writing the brief or PRFAQ.

## Goal
Produce structured market understanding that can feed the canonical BMad planning artifacts.

## Procedure
1. Define the target user, segment, and problem space.
2. Research competitors, substitutes, and incumbent workflows.
3. Capture pricing, differentiation, and adoption constraints.
4. Summarize risks, opportunities, and unresolved questions.
5. Feed the findings into `bmad-product-brief` or `bmad-prfaq` rather than leaving them as loose notes.

## Completion Output
Report key findings, strongest differentiators, market risks, and recommended next workflow.
