# BMad Adversarial Review

## When to Use
Use before finalizing a plan, document, or deliverable when a rigorous challenge pass is warranted.

## Goal
Find weak assumptions, hidden risks, and unjustified claims before they become workflow truth.

## Procedure
1. Identify the target artifact or claim set.
2. Challenge assumptions, ambiguities, missing evidence, and optimistic leaps.
3. Distinguish factual issues from stylistic preferences.
4. Record findings clearly and proportionally.
5. Route corrections back into the relevant BMad workflow.

## Completion Output
Report the main adversarial findings, severity, and recommended correction path.
