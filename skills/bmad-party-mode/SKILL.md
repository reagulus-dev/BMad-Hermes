# BMad Party Mode

## When to Use
Use when a decision benefits from multiple structured perspectives rather than a single straight-line workflow.

## Goal
Generate a bounded, useful multi-perspective discussion without losing decision traceability.

## Procedure
1. Define the question and the viewpoints needed.
2. Keep the discussion scoped and evidence-seeking.
3. Summarize consensus, disagreements, and open questions.
4. Convert the outcome into a concrete next workflow or artifact update.
5. Avoid endless roleplay with no operational result.

## Completion Output
Report the viewpoints considered, points of agreement/disagreement, and recommended next action.
