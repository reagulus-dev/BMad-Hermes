# BMad Domain Research

## When to Use
Use during analysis when the project touches a specialized domain and the team needs grounded vocabulary and constraints.

## Goal
Reduce conceptual mistakes by grounding later artifacts in real domain knowledge.

## Procedure
1. Identify the domain boundaries and the most important actors, objects, and workflows.
2. Research terminology, constraints, regulations, and edge conditions.
3. Capture domain-specific failure modes and non-obvious rules.
4. Summarize the implications for product framing and requirements.
5. Feed the results into `bmad-product-brief`, `bmad-prfaq`, or the PRD rather than leaving them implicit.

## Completion Output
Report essential domain rules, terminology, edge cases, risks, and recommended next workflow.
